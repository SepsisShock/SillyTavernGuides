What does Continue Postfix do?

Sometimes the AI stops writing before you're ready. You press Continue to get more of the same reply.

Continue Postfix adds a small bit of spacing to the beginning of that new text. This helps control how the new writing joins the previous writing. It doesn't change the story or tell the AI what to say.

See the difference

Imagine Homelander is showing Anya how to bake a cake. He stops in the middle of his explanation, so you press Continue. Try the options to see how the text is joined.

The postfix goes immediately before the newly generated text. In this example, you can see the difference in the final combined reply.

Which should I use?

OptionWhat it addsWhen it helps
OptionNoneWhat it addsNothingWhen it helpsThe original reply stopped mid-word, and the new text needs to attach directly.
OptionSpaceWhat it addsOne spaceWhen it helpsYou want the writing to continue on the same line.
OptionNewlineWhat it addsOne line breakWhen it helpsYou want the new text to start on the next line.
OptionDouble newlineWhat it addsTwo line breaksWhen it helpsYou want a blank line between paragraphs.

For most ordinary continuations, Space is a reasonable starting point. If the AI commonly stops in the middle of words, None may fit better.

One small catch

A postfix can't fix every awkward continuation. The AI might repeat itself, change direction, or generate its own spaces and line breaks. These options only control the extra text that SillyTavern inserts.

Continue Nudge vs. Continue Postfix

These two settings sound similar, but they have different jobs.

Continue Nudge

Instructions sent to the AI to encourage it to keep writing.

Continue Postfix

Spacing added before the newly generated continuation text.

Think of Continue Nudge as telling the AI, “Keep going,” while Continue Postfix decides whether its new writing should begin with a space or a line break.
