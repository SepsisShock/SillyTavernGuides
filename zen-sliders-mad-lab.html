Zen Sliders & Mad Lab Mode

Two settings that change how you adjust AI generation parameters in SillyTavern.

Ever opened your AI settings and seen sliders for things like Temperature, Top P, or response length? These controls let you choose numbers that SillyTavern sends to your AI backend when you generate a reply.

Zen Sliders changes how easy those numbers are to adjust. Mad Lab Mode changes which numbers SillyTavern lets you enter.

Neither option automatically makes your AI write better. They're mainly there to give you a different way to work with the settings.

Zen Sliders

Maybe you don't want to fiddle with a slider to move from 0.80 to 0.81, then 0.82, and so on. You'd rather have fewer steps to move through.

That's what Zen Sliders is for. It provides a simpler interface for adjusting sampling parameters, with fewer increments.

What's different?

Why use it? If you're tired of making very small slider adjustments, Zen Sliders may feel more convenient. If you prefer fine control, stick with the regular sliders.

Switching to Zen Sliders by itself doesn't change your AI's behavior. The values you choose are what matter.

Mad Lab Mode

Say you're trying to enter a number in SillyTavern, but the control won't let you go that high. Mad Lab Mode removes SillyTavern's usual numeric limits so you can try that number anyway.

Here's why that might be useful. Imagine your AI backend supports a response length of 8,192 tokens, but the SillyTavern control you're adjusting only goes up to 2,048. You know the backend supports more, yet the normal control is stopping you from entering it.

What changes in that situation?

Normal control allows up to2,048
Mad Lab lets you try8,192

These numbers are just an example. Actual limits depend on the setting, SillyTavern version, and backend.

That's the whole point of Mad Lab Mode: it gets SillyTavern's normal input limit out of your way.

One important catch: Mad Lab Mode removes SillyTavern's limits, not your AI backend's limits. If the backend doesn't support the value you enter, it may reject the request, clamp the value, or behave differently than expected.

When would you actually need this? Mostly when you already know your backend supports a value that SillyTavern's normal controls won't allow. If you haven't run into that problem, you probably don't need Mad Lab Mode.

Which one should I use?

Your situationWhat to choose
The normal controls work fine for me.Neither
I want fewer steps when adjusting sliders.Zen Sliders
I need to enter a value outside SillyTavern's usual range.Mad Lab Mode
I want the AI to write better without changing any values.Neither does that on its own

You can't use Zen Sliders and Mad Lab Mode at the same time. They're alternative control modes, so choose the one that fits what you're trying to do.

Where do I find them?

Open User Settings in SillyTavern and look for Zen Sliders and Mad Lab Mode under the UI customization options. If you're happy with the normal controls, there's nothing you need to change.

For most beginners, I'd leave Mad Lab Mode off until you have a specific reason to use it.
